\caption{Study 1: structural information, identification, and closed-loop tracking.
(a) One-step prediction RMSE on the same independent, noise-free
600-transition evaluation record after 25, 50, 100, and 200 identification
transitions. Symbols and bars summarize the 40 paired noisy fits by their
median and interquartile range. The logarithmic axis excludes the known-model
reference, whose held-out RMSE is $2.82 \times 10^{-17}$ (numerical roundoff).
(b) Original whole-run tracking RMSE over $0 \leq t < 120$ s for
40 paired noisy closed-loop trials, with all reference transitions retained.
Symbols denote medians and bars the interquartile ranges; some ranges are
narrower than the symbols. Parentheses give the number of estimated
coefficients; the known model performs no identification.
Shared and Incorrect sharing impose the correct and opposite-sign coupling
relations, respectively; Relaxed estimates the coupling coefficients
independently, and Quadratic uses the complete quadratic dictionary.
The correctly shared model improves prediction at fixed coefficient count
and improves tracking relative to the affine model. Additional coefficients
do not improve held-out prediction here and yield little further reduction
in whole-run tracking RMSE.}
